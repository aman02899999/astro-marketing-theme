Writing a business plan used to take weeks and cost thousands in consultant fees. With the right ChatGPT prompts, you can produce a solid first draft in a single afternoon — then spend your time refining, not staring at a blank page.

This guide gives you **prompt-by-prompt coverage of every section of a standard business plan**, from executive summary to financial projections.

> **Important:** Use these prompts to generate structure and drafts. Then replace placeholder data with your own real numbers, market research, and competitive intelligence. AI drafts business plans — you validate them.

## Executive Summary Prompts

### Prompt 1: Executive Summary Generator

```
Write an executive summary for my business plan.

Business name: [name]
Business type: [product / service / SaaS / marketplace / etc.]
Problem being solved: [specific problem]
Solution: [your product/service and how it solves the problem]
Target market: [who your customers are, with size estimate]
Revenue model: [how you make money]
Traction so far: [revenue, users, pilots, partnerships — or "pre-revenue"]
Funding ask (if any): [amount and what it's for]

Write a 200–300 word executive summary that a busy investor would read in 60 seconds
and understand exactly what we do, why it matters, and why now.
```

---

### Prompt 2: Mission and Vision Statements

```
Write a mission statement and vision statement for:

Company: [name]
What we do: [one sentence]
Who we serve: [target customer]
Why it matters: [the broader impact]
Company values: [list 3–5 core values]

Mission statement: what we do and for whom (present tense, 1–2 sentences)
Vision statement: the world we're working toward (aspirational, 1–2 sentences)

Avoid: corporate jargon, vague buzzwords, anything that could apply to any company.
```

## Market Analysis Prompts

### Prompt 3: Market Analysis Framework

```
Help me write a market analysis section for my business plan.

Industry: [your industry]
Geography: [local / national / global]
Target customer: [detailed description]

Structure the analysis covering:
1. Total Addressable Market (TAM) — methodology and estimate
2. Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM) — our realistic target
3. Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM) — year 1–3 capture
4. Market trends driving growth (list 3–5)
5. Market challenges and risks (list 3)
6. Customer segmentation (2–3 primary segments)

Note where I need to insert real research data with [DATA NEEDED: source type].
```

---

### Prompt 4: Competitive Analysis

```
Help me write a competitive analysis for:

My business: [description]
My main competitors: [list 3–5 by name or type]
My key differentiator: [what makes us different]

For each competitor, analyze:
- Their core offering
- Their pricing model
- Their target customer
- Their main strengths
- Their main weaknesses

Then produce:
- A competitive positioning matrix (text format)
- Our competitive advantage summary (2–3 sentences)
- Moat or defensibility explanation
```

## Operations and Strategy Prompts

### Prompt 5: Go-to-Market Strategy

```
Write a go-to-market strategy for:

Product: [description]
Target customer: [detailed profile]
Geographic focus: [where you're launching first]
Budget for launch: [$X or "bootstrap"]
Timeline: [launch in X months]

Include:
1. Launch channels (ranked by priority and cost)
2. Customer acquisition strategy for first 100 customers
3. Pricing strategy with rationale
4. Partnerships or distribution channels to pursue
5. Key metrics to track in first 90 days
6. 30/60/90 day milestone plan
```

---

### Prompt 6: SWOT Analysis

```
Generate a detailed SWOT analysis for:

Business: [name and description]
Stage: [pre-revenue / early stage / growth / established]
Market: [your market]
Key resources: [what you have — team, IP, capital, relationships]

For each quadrant provide 5 specific, concrete points (not generic):
- Strengths: internal advantages we currently have
- Weaknesses: internal gaps or limitations
- Opportunities: external trends or gaps we can exploit
- Threats: external risks that could hurt the business

After the SWOT, suggest the top 3 strategic priorities based on the analysis.
```

## Financial Projections Prompts

### Prompt 7: Revenue Model Design

```
Help me design a revenue model for:

Business type: [description]
Product/service: [what you sell]
Target customer: [who buys]
Pricing options I'm considering: [list options or ask for recommendations]

Evaluate 3 possible revenue models for my business:
1. [model 1, e.g. subscription]
2. [model 2, e.g. one-time purchase]
3. [model 3, e.g. transaction fee]

For each model:
- How it works
- Pros and cons for my specific business
- Unit economics (per customer)
- Scalability

Recommend the best model and explain why.
```

---

### Prompt 8: 3-Year Financial Projection Framework

```
Build a 3-year financial projection framework for:

Business: [description]
Revenue model: [subscription / per-unit / service / etc.]
Average revenue per customer: [$X / month or year]
Assumed customer growth rate: [X% month-over-month or provide estimates]
Key costs: [list main cost categories]
Current team size: [X people]
Planned hiring: [describe growth plan]

Produce:
- Year 1, 2, 3 revenue projections
- Gross margin estimate
- Key operating expenses by category
- Headcount plan
- Break-even analysis
- Cash flow summary narrative

Flag every assumption clearly so I can replace with real numbers.
```

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can ChatGPT write a complete business plan ready to submit to investors?

As a first draft, yes. As a final product, no. ChatGPT generates structure, language, and frameworks. The numbers, market data, and validation must come from you and real research. Sophisticated investors will spot generic AI content instantly — use it to get to 70% done, then put in the work on the rest.

### What sections should every business plan include?

Executive summary, company overview, problem and solution, market analysis, competitive analysis, product/service description, go-to-market strategy, operations plan, team bios, financial projections, and funding ask (if applicable). Not all plans need every section — a lean plan for internal use is much shorter.

### How do I make the financial projections realistic?

Start with bottom-up assumptions: how many sales calls your team can make, your realistic close rate, your average contract value. Build up from there rather than top-down from market size. ChatGPT can build the model — you supply the assumptions.

### Should I use ChatGPT for a business plan if I'm raising funding?

Use it to draft, organize, and structure. Then thoroughly validate every claim with real data. Have a human advisor review it before sending to investors. The business model, market insight, and traction need to be genuinely yours.

### What's the most important section of a business plan?

For investors: the team section and the financial projections. For internal planning: the go-to-market strategy and unit economics. For a bank loan: financial history, collateral, and repayment plan.
