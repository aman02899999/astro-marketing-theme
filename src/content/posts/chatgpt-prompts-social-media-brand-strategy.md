Posting on social media without a strategy is activity, not progress. The brands that grow consistently have a clear voice, a defined audience, and content pillars that make every post purposeful. These prompts build all three.

## Strategy Foundation Prompts

### Prompt 1: Social Media Strategy Builder

```
Build a social media strategy for:

Brand: [company name and what you sell]
Business goal: [what you need social media to achieve — leads / sales / awareness / retention]
Target audience: [detailed customer description]
Platforms to focus on: [choose 2–3 max]
Resources: [team size, content creation capability, budget]
Competitors' social presence: [describe or ask ChatGPT to infer from the niche]

Strategy document:

1. AUDIENCE DEFINITION
   - Primary audience persona (detailed)
   - What they want from social media content (entertainment / education / inspiration / community)
   - Where they spend time and when

2. CONTENT PILLARS (3–4 recurring themes)
   - Pillar 1: [educational — builds authority]
   - Pillar 2: [emotional — builds connection]
   - Pillar 3: [promotional — drives action]
   - Pillar 4: [behind-the-scenes — builds trust]

3. VOICE AND TONE
   - 3 personality adjectives
   - 3 phrases we use / 3 we never use
   - Tone variations per platform

4. METRICS AND GOALS
   - 90-day targets per platform
   - Weekly metrics to track

5. CONTENT CALENDAR FRAMEWORK
   - Post types per day of week
   - Ratio: value content vs. promotional content
```

### Prompt 2: Competitor Social Analysis

```
Analyze the social media presence of competitors in the [industry] space:

My brand: [description]
Competitors to analyze (describe their social presence):
1. [Competitor 1]: [what they post, their tone, approximate following]
2. [Competitor 2]: [what they post, their tone, approximate following]
3. [Competitor 3]: [what they post, their tone, approximate following]

Analyze:
1. What content types perform best for each competitor
2. What gaps or underserved topics none of them cover
3. What tone or voice position is available (nobody is doing [X])
4. Which competitor's strategy is most effective and why
5. Where my brand can be meaningfully different
6. 3 content ideas only I can execute (based on my unique position)
```

## Campaign Planning Prompts

### Prompt 3: Social Media Campaign Planner

```
Plan a social media campaign for:

Campaign goal: [specific, measurable — e.g. "generate 200 leads for X product launch"]
Duration: [X weeks]
Budget: [$X — or organic only]
Platforms: [list]
Audience: [targeting description]
Key message: [what you want them to know/feel/do]

Campaign plan:

PRE-LAUNCH (Week 1):
- Teaser content strategy
- Audience warm-up posts
- Influencer or partner activation plan

LAUNCH WEEK:
- Launch day content schedule (hour by hour if relevant)
- Paid amplification if budget
- Engagement response plan

SUSTAIN (Weeks 2–X):
- Content variety to maintain momentum
- Retargeting messaging
- Social proof content (user-generated, testimonials)

POST-CAMPAIGN:
- Results analysis framework
- Retargeting audience for future campaigns
```

## Analytics and Optimization Prompts

### Prompt 4: Content Performance Analyzer

```
Help me analyze my social media performance:

Platform: [which platform]
Time period: [last X weeks/months]
Metrics I have:
- Top 5 posts by reach/impressions: [paste titles/descriptions]
- Bottom 5 posts by reach: [paste]
- Engagement rate average: [X%]
- Follower growth: [+X per week]
- Click-through rate: [X%]
- Best performing content type: [from your data]

Analysis:
1. What patterns appear in the top-performing content?
2. What's missing or wrong with the low performers?
3. What content type should I make more of?
4. What should I stop making?
5. The one change that would most improve my performance
6. 3 content ideas based on what's working

Base recommendations only on patterns in the data I've provided.
```

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many social media platforms should a brand focus on?

Two to three, maximum, until you have dedicated resources for more. Spreading across 6 platforms produces mediocre presence everywhere instead of strong presence somewhere. Choose based on where your ideal customers actually spend time. For most B2B brands: LinkedIn + one other. For B2C: Instagram or TikTok + one other. Master two before adding a third.

### What's the right ratio of promotional to non-promotional content?

The old "80/20 rule" (80% value, 20% promotional) remains a good starting point. More precisely: your promotional content will only work if your value content has built an audience that trusts you. If every third post is "buy now," you'll lose followers faster than you gain them. Think of it as: earn the right to promote with every non-promotional post.

### How do I maintain consistent social media presence as a solo founder?

Batch content creation — dedicate 3 hours per week to creating all content for the next week. Repurpose relentlessly (one blog post → 5 social posts). Use scheduling tools (Buffer, Later, Hootsuite). Accept that "good and consistent" beats "perfect and sporadic." 3 posts per week every week outperforms 10 posts in a burst then silence.

### What's the biggest social media strategy mistake?

Optimizing for vanity metrics (likes, followers) instead of business metrics (leads, sales, customer conversations). A post that generates 500 likes from people who'll never buy from you is worth less than a post that generates 20 clicks to your product page. Define your business goal first (Prompt 1), then track metrics that connect to it.

### When should I start using paid social media ads?

When your organic content has validated what resonates with your audience. Paid amplifies what already works — it doesn't fix content that doesn't resonate organically. Have 3–5 content formats with proven engagement before putting budget behind them. Then use paid to reach more people who look like your organic engagers.
