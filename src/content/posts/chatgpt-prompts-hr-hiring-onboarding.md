HR professionals who use AI spend more time on the high-value human work — culture, relationships, judgment calls — and less on documentation. These prompts eliminate hours of writing from every stage of the employee lifecycle.

## Recruitment Prompts

### Prompt 1: Job Description Writer

```
Write a compelling job description for:

Role: [job title]
Company: [brief company description]
Team: [which team this person joins]
Location: [remote / on-site / hybrid — location]
Salary range: [include or omit based on your policy]

The ideal candidate:
- [Core competency 1]
- [Core competency 2]
- [Core competency 3]
Key responsibilities: [list 5–7]
Must-have requirements: [list 3–5]
Nice-to-have: [list 2–3]

Tone: [startup-casual / corporate / technical / mission-driven]

Requirements:
- Lead with what the person will DO and BUILD, not just a list of skills
- Include DEI-forward language (audit for coded language)
- Avoid: "rockstar", "ninja", "hustle", "fast-paced" (unless genuinely accurate)
- Include a genuine company culture statement (not boilerplate)
```

### Prompt 2: Interview Question Bank

```
Create a structured interview question bank for:

Role: [job title]
Seniority: [junior / mid / senior / lead]
Key competencies to assess: [list 5–6 from the job description]

For each competency provide:
- 2 behavioral questions (STAR format: "Tell me about a time...")
- 1 situational question ("What would you do if...")
- 1 technical or role-specific question
- Follow-up probes for each
- What "strong" vs. "weak" answers look like

Also provide:
- 3 culture-fit questions (not cliché)
- 2 motivation and values questions
- 1 question to assess growth mindset
- Questions to avoid (legally problematic or biased)
```

---

### Prompt 3: Structured Interview Scorecard

```
Create a structured interview scorecard for hiring a [role]:

Competencies to evaluate: [list from job description]
Interview format: [panel / sequential / single interviewer]
Rating scale: [1–5 or 1–4 to avoid central tendency]

For each competency:
- Competency name and definition
- Interview questions to ask
- Observable indicators at each rating level (what does a "3" look like vs. a "5"?)
- Weight (percentage of total score)

Total scorecard should allow comparison between candidates on the same criteria.
Include: overall recommendation field (Hire / No Hire / Hold) with justification space.
```

## Onboarding Prompts

### Prompt 4: 90-Day Onboarding Plan

```
Create a 90-day onboarding plan for a new [role]:

Company type: [startup / enterprise / agency / etc.]
Department: [team]
Manager: [their manager's role]
Key tools and systems: [list what they'll use]
Success metric at 90 days: [what does "good" look like?]

Structure:
Day 1: First day agenda (hour by hour)
Week 1: Orientation goals and activities
Weeks 2–4: Learning and relationship building
Month 2: First independent contributions
Month 3: Full ramp, first performance conversation

For each phase:
- Goals
- Scheduled activities and meetings
- Resources to review
- Milestones and checkpoints
- Manager actions required
```

---

### Prompt 5: Onboarding Email Sequence

```
Write an onboarding email sequence for new employees:

Company: [brief description]
Role type: [the role being onboarded]
Culture: [3 adjectives]

Email 1 (Pre-start, 1 week before): Welcome + what to expect on day 1
Email 2 (Day 1 morning): Here's your day 1 schedule + who to contact
Email 3 (End of week 1): Check-in + first week reflection questions
Email 4 (End of month 1): 30-day review prep + what comes next
Email 5 (Day 85): 90-day review prep

Each email: subject, body (under 200 words), clear next action.
```

## Performance Management Prompts

### Prompt 6: Performance Review Template

```
Create a performance review template for:

Company type: [startup / corporate / non-profit]
Review cycle: [annual / semi-annual / quarterly]
Rating scale: [exceeds expectations / meets / below / etc. or numeric]
Format: [manager-written / 360 / self-assessment + manager]

Sections to include:
1. Role responsibilities assessment (how well did they do the job)
2. Goals from last period: set → achieved → rating
3. Core competencies assessment (communication, collaboration, etc.)
4. Strengths narrative (specific examples)
5. Development areas (growth-framed, not punitive)
6. Goals for next period (SMART format)
7. Career development conversation notes
8. Overall rating and justification

Include guidance for managers on writing honest, specific, legally sound reviews.
```

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can ChatGPT help write legally compliant job descriptions?

ChatGPT can audit descriptions for common problematic language (age-coded terms, requirements that screen out protected classes, etc.). However, employment law varies by jurisdiction and changes frequently. Always have final job descriptions reviewed by an HR attorney or experienced HR leader before publishing.

### How do I avoid bias in AI-generated interview questions?

Use Prompt 2 and explicitly ask it to flag legally problematic questions. Generally avoid questions about: family status, national origin, age, religion, or anything that isn't directly related to job performance. Behavioral and situational questions (STAR format) are the most legally defensible and most predictive of performance.

### What's the ROI of structured interviews?

Research consistently shows structured interviews (same questions, same scoring, multiple evaluators) are 2–3x more predictive of job performance than unstructured interviews. The scorecard in Prompt 3 makes structured interviewing easy to implement.

### How do I personalize AI-generated onboarding plans?

Generate the framework with Prompt 4, then customize: (1) add specific people the new hire needs to meet, (2) insert company-specific tools and processes, (3) tailor the milestones to the specific role's first deliverables. The AI-generated plan handles the structure; you handle the specifics.

### Can I use ChatGPT for sensitive HR situations like PIPs or terminations?

Use it as a drafting tool with extreme caution. Performance improvement plans and termination documentation have legal implications and must follow your company's specific procedures and local employment law. Use AI to draft, then have HR leadership and legal review everything before it's used.
