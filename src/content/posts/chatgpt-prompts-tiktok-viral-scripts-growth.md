TikTok is the most powerful organic reach platform available to creators in 2025. The algorithm doesn't care about your follower count — it cares about watch time, completion rate, and shares. These prompts help you engineer both.

## TikTok Script Prompts

### Prompt 1: Viral TikTok Script

```
Write a TikTok video script:

Topic: "[your topic]"
Niche: [your niche]
Video length: [15–30s / 30–60s / 1–3 min]
Format: [talking head / voiceover / text-on-screen / POV / tutorial / storytelling]
Target viewer: [who you're making this for]
Goal: [followers / shares / saves / comments / profile visits]

TikTok script rules:
- Hook: First 1–2 seconds determines if they watch (start mid-story or mid-action)
- Retention pattern: new information or visual change every 3–5 seconds
- Loop potential: end where you started (encourages replay)
- CTA: one action only (follow / comment / share — not all three)

Include:
- Spoken script
- [ON-SCREEN TEXT] cues
- [VISUAL ACTION] notes
- [SOUND/MUSIC NOTE] suggestions
- Timing marks
```

### Prompt 2: Hook Line Generator for TikTok

```
Write 20 TikTok hook lines for: "[topic]"

TikTok hooks must work in the FIRST 2 SECONDS — before the viewer can swipe.

Hook categories (4 each):
1. "POV:" hooks ("POV: you just discovered [thing]")
2. "Things nobody tells you about [X]" format
3. Direct address ("If you [situation], stop scrolling")
4. Cliffhanger ("I tried [thing] for 30 days. Here's what happened:")
5. Controversy or hot take ("Unpopular opinion: [statement]")

For each hook: rate scroll-stop potential 1–10 and explain why it works or doesn't.
Recommend top 5 to film first.
```

### Prompt 3: Series Concept Generator

```
Design a TikTok series concept for:

Niche: [your topic]
Posting frequency: [X videos per week]
My expertise: [what I know well]
Audience: [who I want to attract]

Create 3 distinct series concepts:

Series 1: Educational/informational
- Series name and premise
- Episode 1–5 outlines
- Why this hooks and retains viewers

Series 2: Storytelling/personal
- Series name and premise
- Narrative arc across 5–10 episodes
- Cliffhanger strategy to drive follow

Series 3: Challenge/trend participation
- How to put my niche spin on trending formats
- 5 execution ideas

For each series: the "follow trigger" — why would someone follow to see more?
```

## TikTok SEO and Strategy Prompts

### Prompt 4: TikTok Caption and Hashtag Strategy

```
Write TikTok captions and hashtag strategies for:

Video about: [your video topic]
Niche: [your niche]
Target audience location: [country/region if relevant]

TikTok caption (under 150 characters — shows before "more"):
- Must add context or intrigue not visible in the video
- Can ask a question to drive comments
- Or reinforce the hook

Hashtag strategy (5–7 total):
- 2 niche-specific hashtags (medium competition)
- 2 topic hashtags (high volume)
- 1 community hashtag
- Optional: 1 trending hashtag if genuinely relevant

Also: the first comment I should post immediately after uploading (many creators use this for extra keyword-rich text).
```

### Prompt 5: TikTok Growth Strategy

```
Build a 90-day TikTok growth strategy for:

Account niche: [topic]
Current followers: [X — or starting from zero]
Goal: [X followers / monetization / drive traffic to [destination]]
Time available: [X videos per week]
Content I can produce: [formats you're comfortable with]

Strategy includes:
1. Content pillars (3 types of content to rotate between)
2. Posting schedule recommendation
3. First 10 video ideas with hooks
4. Engagement tactics (how to use duets, stitches, replies)
5. Sound/trend participation strategy
6. Collaborations to pursue
7. Analytics to track weekly
8. 30-day / 60-day / 90-day milestones

Be specific. I don't need "post consistently" — I need "post [X] at [Y] time."
```

## Stitch and Duet Prompts

### Prompt 6: Stitch Response Script

```
I want to stitch this TikTok to respond to/add to it:

Original video summary: [describe the video and its claim or question]
My response angle: [agree and add / respectfully disagree / add expert context / answer the question]
My expertise: [why I'm qualified to respond]
Video length: [under 60 seconds]

Script:
1. First 2 seconds: establish why I'm responding (context cue)
2. My response (concise, adds genuine value)
3. One specific new insight the original didn't cover
4. CTA: invite them to follow for more on this topic

Tone: respectful, not combative. Adding value > winning arguments.
```

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When is the best time to post on TikTok?

TikTok distributes content based on engagement signals, not posting time, more than any other platform. That said, posting when your specific audience is active improves early engagement signals. Check TikTok Analytics → Followers → Activity for your audience's peak hours. Generally: 6–9 AM and 7–10 PM local time perform well for most niches.

### How many hashtags should I use on TikTok?

5–7 strategic hashtags outperform 20+ generic ones. TikTok now uses AI topic categorization more heavily than hashtags for content distribution. Use hashtags to signal your niche to the algorithm, not as a volume game. Prompt 4 gives you the right framework.

### Does posting frequency matter on TikTok?

More than any other platform for growth. The algorithm needs data to learn what kind of account you are and who to show you to. During your first 60 days, 1–2 videos per day is ideal if you can maintain quality. After establishing your niche signal, 5–7 quality videos per week sustains growth. Consistency matters more than perfection.

### Why do some TikToks go viral with no followers?

TikTok shows every new video to a small test group regardless of follower count. If early engagement (watch-through rate, comments, shares) exceeds expectations for that group, it's shown to progressively larger groups. This is why hook quality (Prompt 2) is the single most important variable — it determines your initial engagement signal.

### How do I recover a TikTok account that stopped getting views?

First, check if you've been shadow-restricted (post one video and wait 48 hours — if zero views, you likely are). If restricted: delete any content that may have violated community guidelines, take a 3-day break, then return with strong high-engagement content. If not restricted, the algorithm just needs a reset — try a new content angle or format. Accounts that get stuck on one format often plateau until they evolve.
