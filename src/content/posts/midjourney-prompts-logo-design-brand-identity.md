Midjourney changed what's possible for brand design. Whether you're a designer using AI to explore concepts faster, or a founder building a brand on a budget, these prompts give you the exact formulas that produce professional-quality logo and brand identity work.

## Understanding the Midjourney Logo Prompt Formula

The most reliable logo prompts follow this structure:

```
[Subject] logo, [style descriptor], [color palette], [mood/feel],
[technical specs], --no [things to exclude] --v 6 --style raw
```

Example:
```
minimalist mountain outdoor brand logo, geometric, deep forest green and warm cream,
adventurous yet refined, vector style, clean lines, white background --no text
gradients photorealistic shadows --v 6 --style raw
```

## Wordmark and Lettermark Prompts

### Prompt 1: Clean Wordmark Logo

```
professional wordmark logo for "[brand name]", [industry] company,
[style: minimal / bold / elegant / modern], [font personality: geometric sans-serif /
humanist / serif / slab], [primary color], white background, vector style,
brand identity concept --no icons symbols marks gradients shadows --v 6
```

**Example filled in:**
```
professional wordmark logo for "Oakfield", architecture firm,
minimal and refined, geometric sans-serif with strong proportions,
deep slate blue and warm white, white background, vector style,
brand identity concept --no icons symbols marks gradients shadows --v 6 --style raw
```

## Style Modifier Reference

Use these modifiers to dial in the exact aesthetic:

**Style descriptors:**
- `flat design vector` — clean, scalable, no gradients
- `bauhaus inspired` — geometric, functional, bold
- `swiss international style` — grid-based, clean typography
- `art deco` — geometric luxury, ornate details
- `brutalist` — bold, raw, high contrast

**Rendering parameters:**
- `--style raw` — less AI "beautification," more literal
- `--stylize 0` — minimal style processing
- `--chaos 10-30` — more variation in outputs
- `--ar 1:1` — square (logos)
- `--ar 16:9` — widescreen (presentations, headers)

**Useful negative prompts (`--no`):**
`text words letters watermark photorealism drop shadows gradients complex backgrounds noise texture`

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I trademark a logo created with Midjourney?

This is a rapidly evolving legal area. As of 2025, AI-generated works have limited copyright protection in the US. Most brand attorneys recommend using AI for concept exploration, then having a human designer finalize and create the production-ready version — which can then be trademarked. Always consult an IP attorney for your specific situation.

### What Midjourney version is best for logos?

Midjourney v6 with `--style raw` produces the most clean, vector-friendly results for logos. The `--style raw` parameter reduces the model's tendency to add photorealistic rendering, which is counterproductive for logo work.

### How do I get consistent colors across Midjourney outputs?

Specify hex codes in your prompt: "using color palette #1A3A5C and #F5F0E8". Midjourney doesn't guarantee exact hex matching, but it dramatically narrows the color range. For precise color control, treat Midjourney outputs as concepts and finalize in Illustrator or Figma.

### What's the best way to get a vector file from Midjourney?

Midjourney outputs raster images (PNG). To get vector files: (1) upscale the best output, (2) use Adobe Illustrator's Image Trace or Vector Magic to auto-trace, (3) clean up manually in Illustrator or Inkscape. For logos, the AI concept + human vectorization workflow consistently produces the best results.

### How many prompts should I generate before choosing a direction?

Generate 20–40 variations before narrowing down. Use Midjourney's `/prefer` option to save your best prompt suffixes. Grid outputs (4 per run) mean 10 runs gives you 40 concepts. Good logo design requires exploring many directions before the right one emerges.
