Finance is where most small business owners feel least confident and spend the most time worrying. These ChatGPT prompts won't replace your accountant — but they'll help you think through financial decisions faster, communicate numbers more clearly, and build planning frameworks you can actually use.

> **Disclaimer:** These prompts are for educational and planning purposes. Always verify financial decisions with a qualified accountant or financial advisor.

## Financial Analysis Prompts

### Prompt 1: Unit Economics Calculator

```
Help me calculate and interpret my unit economics:

Business model: [subscription / e-commerce / service / marketplace]
Revenue per customer: [$X per month/year or per transaction]
Cost to acquire one customer (CAC): [$X — or help me calculate it]
Monthly churn rate: [X% — or "don't know"]
Variable cost per customer: [$X per month/year]
Fixed costs per month: [$X]

Calculate:
1. Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
2. LTV:CAC ratio (target >3:1)
3. CAC payback period (months to recover acquisition cost)
4. Gross margin per customer
5. Contribution margin
6. Break-even customers needed

Interpret each metric: what does this tell me about my business health?
Flag any ratios that indicate a problem and explain why.
```

### Prompt 2: Pricing Strategy Analyzer

```
Help me set or validate my pricing:

Product/service: [description]
My costs (variable per unit): [$X]
My fixed costs per month: [$X]
Current price: [$X — or "not set yet"]
Competitor pricing: [what competitors charge]
Target customer: [description — their budget sensitivity]
Business goal: [volume / margin / market share]

Analyze 3 pricing strategies for my situation:
1. Cost-plus pricing
2. Value-based pricing
3. Competitive positioning pricing

For each:
- Recommended price point
- Gross margin at that price
- Minimum volume to break even
- Pros and cons for my specific situation
- Who this strategy attracts vs. repels

Recommend the best approach and explain why.
```

### Prompt 3: Cash Flow Narrative

```
Help me understand and explain my cash flow situation:

Monthly revenue: [$X]
Monthly expenses breakdown:
  - [Category]: [$X]
  - [Category]: [$X]
  - [Category]: [$X]
Cash in bank: [$X]
Accounts receivable outstanding: [$X]
Accounts payable outstanding: [$X]
Upcoming large expenses: [list with amounts and dates]
Revenue trend: [growing X% / flat / declining X%]

Produce:
1. Current cash runway (months before cash runs out at current burn)
2. The 3 biggest risks to cash flow right now
3. The 2 fastest levers to improve cash position
4. A plain-English narrative for my co-founder / board about cash health
5. Questions I should be asking my accountant
```

## Budget and Planning Prompts

### Prompt 4: Annual Budget Template

```
Create an annual budget template for a [type of business]:

Revenue streams: [list all ways you make money]
Team size: [X people]
Stage: [pre-revenue / early / growth / established]
Key upcoming investments: [list major planned expenses]

Build a 12-month budget framework with:
1. Revenue projections (with assumptions clearly labeled)
2. Cost of Goods Sold / Cost of Revenue
3. Gross Profit and Margin
4. Operating Expenses by category
5. EBITDA
6. One-time vs. recurring cost distinction
7. Monthly cash flow projection
8. Key assumptions for each line item

Format as a table. Mark all assumptions so I can update them with real numbers.
```

### Prompt 5: Investor Update Writer

```
Write a monthly investor update for:

Company: [name]
Month: [month/year]
Stage: [seed / Series A / bootstrapped]

This month's highlights:
[paste your raw notes — rough is fine]

Financial metrics:
- Revenue: [$X] ([X%] vs last month)
- MRR/ARR: [$X]
- Burn rate: [$X/month]
- Runway: [X months]
- Key metric for my business: [X]

Challenges this month: [honest description]

Next month focus: [top 3 priorities]

Write a professional, transparent investor update that:
- Leads with the most important number
- Is honest about challenges (investors respect candor)
- Shows clear next steps
- Reads in under 3 minutes
- Builds confidence without overselling
```

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can ChatGPT do my financial modeling?

It can build the framework and logic, but you must provide the real numbers and assumptions. Think of it as a very fast spreadsheet assistant that can also explain financial concepts. Never submit AI-generated financial models to investors or lenders without thorough review by a qualified financial professional.

### What's the most useful financial prompt for a first-time founder?

Prompt 1 (Unit Economics). Most early-stage founders don't know their LTV:CAC ratio or their CAC payback period — these are the two most important metrics for understanding whether your business model is fundamentally sound. Knowing these numbers changes every growth decision you make.

### How do I use ChatGPT for fundraising preparation?

Use Prompt 3 (Cash Flow Narrative) to stress-test your story before investor meetings. Use Prompt 4 (Budget Template) to build your financial model framework. Ask ChatGPT to role-play as a skeptical investor and challenge your assumptions. The preparation value is enormous even if ChatGPT can't replace a financial advisor.

### Is ChatGPT reliable for pricing decisions?

For the framework and options analysis, yes. For the final number, no — that requires real market data, customer conversations, and competitive intelligence that ChatGPT doesn't have. Use Prompt 2 to understand the decision dimensions, then validate your final price with real customer feedback before committing.

### How do I get ChatGPT to explain financial concepts I don't understand?

Ask it to explain at your level: "Explain [concept] to me like I'm a smart non-accountant founder. Give me one concrete example from a SaaS business." The Feynman-style explanation request ("explain it simply, then build up") is extremely effective for financial literacy building.
