Affiliate marketing at scale requires three things: content that ranks, reviews readers trust, and a conversion-optimized funnel. These prompts build all three systematically.

## Niche Research Prompts

### Prompt 1: Affiliate Niche Analyzer

```
Analyze these niches for affiliate marketing potential:

Niches to evaluate: [list 5–10]

For each niche score and explain:
1. Commission potential (high-ticket / recurring / volume)
2. Competition level (can a new site compete?)
3. Search intent alignment (do people buy in this niche from content?)
4. Product availability (are there affiliate programs worth joining?)
5. Content scalability (can I produce 100+ articles on this topic?)
6. Evergreen vs. trend (will this still be relevant in 3 years?)

Recommend top 3 niches with the best commission/competition ratio.
For each: the 3 highest-commission affiliate programs to join.
```

### Prompt 2: Affiliate Content Strategy

```
Build an affiliate content strategy for a site about: [niche]

Target audience: [who searches in this niche]
Primary monetization: [affiliate commissions / display ads / both]
Domain age: [new / established]

Content architecture:

MONEY PAGES (high commercial intent):
- Best [product category] list posts
- [Product A] vs [Product B] comparisons
- [Product] review pages

INFORMATIONAL PAGES (drive organic traffic):
- How-to guides that build topical authority
- Problem/solution articles that funnel to money pages

PILLAR PAGES:
- 2–3 comprehensive guides that capture broad traffic

For each content type:
- Example title and target keyword
- Internal linking strategy (how it feeds the money pages)
- Conversion approach (where to place affiliate links)
```

## Review Content Prompts

### Prompt 3: Affiliate Product Review Template

```
Write an affiliate product review for: [product name]

Product category: [type]
My testing experience: [how I used it or describe based on research]
Target reader: [who is considering buying this]
Main affiliate program: [Amazon / direct / other]

Review structure:
1. VERDICT UPFRONT: [score/10 + one-sentence summary — eliminates bounce from impatient readers]
2. WHO IT'S FOR (and who should look elsewhere)
3. KEY FEATURES with honest assessment (not spec sheet)
4. WHAT I LIKED (specific, with examples)
5. WHAT I DIDN'T LIKE (be honest — builds trust)
6. COMPARISON to 2 main alternatives (brief)
7. PRICING and value assessment
8. FINAL VERDICT with affiliate CTA

Rules: specific beats vague. "The battery lasts 11 hours" beats "excellent battery life."
Affiliate link placement: 3–5 times (start, after pros, after comparison, final CTA).
```

### Prompt 4: Comparison Page Writer

```
Write a comparison page for: "[Product A] vs [Product B]"

Target keyword: "[product A] vs [product B]"
Target reader: [someone deciding between these two options]
My recommendation: [which is better overall and why]
Primary affiliate relationship: [which product pays more — disclose this]

Structure:
1. Quick answer (for skimmers): "Choose [A] if [X], choose [B] if [Y]"
2. Side-by-side comparison table (5–8 key criteria)
3. [Product A] in-depth (pros, cons, best for)
4. [Product B] in-depth (pros, cons, best for)
5. Head-to-head on the criteria that matter most to buyers
6. Who should choose [A] (specific use case)
7. Who should choose [B] (specific use case)
8. Final verdict with affiliate links

Be genuinely helpful. Readers sense when a comparison is rigged toward commission.
```

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What's the highest-commission affiliate niche?

Financial products (credit cards, investing platforms, insurance) offer $50–$200+ per conversion. SaaS software offers 20–40% recurring monthly commissions. High-ticket physical products (furniture, appliances, outdoor equipment) earn 3–8% of large order values. The best niche is the intersection of high commission, traffic you can get, and content you can produce credibly.

### How long until affiliate marketing generates income?

Realistic timeline for a content-first affiliate site: 6–12 months before meaningful traffic, 12–18 months before replacing a part-time income, 24+ months for a full-time income. Sites that succeed are those with a 2-year commitment from day one. Quick-flip tactics (thin sites, manipulative tactics) get hit by Google algorithm updates — long-term content quality wins.

### Should I disclose affiliate relationships?

Yes, always — it's legally required (FTC in the US, ASA in the UK, similar regulators elsewhere). More importantly, it builds reader trust. "This article contains affiliate links. If you buy through them, I earn a small commission at no cost to you" is all you need. Readers who trust you convert at far higher rates than readers who feel manipulated.

### What's the biggest affiliate marketing mistake?

Prioritizing commission rate over product quality. Recommending a mediocre product because it pays more than the genuinely better alternative destroys trust when readers have a bad experience. Long-term: a reputation for honest recommendations drives more revenue than short-term commission optimization.

### How many articles do I need before an affiliate site gets traffic?

Topical authority matters more than raw article count. A site with 50 highly relevant, deeply useful articles in a focused niche outperforms a site with 500 thin articles across broad topics. Aim for depth in your niche cluster (Prompt 2) before expanding breadth.
