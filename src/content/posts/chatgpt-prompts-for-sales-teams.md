Sales reps who use AI don't just send more emails — they send better ones. They walk into calls more prepared, handle objections faster, and spend less time on admin. These 30 ChatGPT sales prompts are used by professionals to compress hours of sales work into minutes.

## Prospecting Prompts

### Prompt 1: Ideal Customer Profile

```
Help me define my Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) for:

Product/service: [what you sell]
Current best customers: [describe your top 3 customers — industry, size, use case]
Deal size: [$X average contract value]
Sales cycle: [X weeks/months]

Define my ICP with:
1. Firmographics (industry, company size, revenue range, geography)
2. Technographics (tech stack signals that indicate a good fit)
3. Buying triggers (events that make them likely to buy now)
4. Decision-maker title and department
5. What they care about vs. what they say they care about
6. Disqualification signals (who to avoid)
```

---

### Prompt 2: Lead Research Brief

```
I'm about to reach out to [company name] in the [industry] space.

Based on what you know about companies like this, help me prepare:
1. Common pain points for a [company type] of this size
2. Likely priorities for a [decision-maker title] right now
3. Questions to ask to diagnose their situation
4. Risks they probably want to avoid
5. How to frame my [product/service] in terms of their priorities

Note: I'll verify all of this before the call. I want hypotheses to test, not facts.
```

---

### Prompt 3: Cold Email Sequence

```
Write a 4-touch cold email sequence for:

Prospect: [title] at [company type]
My product: [one sentence]
Pain I'm solving: [specific pain]
Social proof: [customer or result I can reference]

Email 1 (day 1): Problem-focused, no pitch, under 80 words
Email 2 (day 4): Case study or insight, soft ask
Email 3 (day 8): Different angle or objection pre-empt
Email 4 (day 14): Short breakup email with a final value add

Each email: subject line, preview text, body, CTA.
```

## Discovery and Qualification Prompts

### Prompt 4: Discovery Call Question Set

```
Generate a discovery call question set for selling [product/service] to [buyer role].

Create 20 questions across these categories:
- Situation (current state, context)
- Problem (pain points, frustrations)
- Implication (impact of the problem)
- Need-payoff (value of solving it)

Mark each question: [OPEN / CLOSED] and [EARLY / MID / LATE] in the call.
Include 3 "trap" questions that reveal whether this is a qualified deal or not.
```

---

### Prompt 5: BANT Qualification Script

```
Write a natural-sounding BANT qualification script for [your product].

Buyer: [title and company type]
Price point: [$X]
Sales cycle: [X weeks]

For each BANT element, give me:
- 2 conversational questions to uncover it
- What a qualified vs. disqualified answer sounds like
- How to handle "I don't know" or a dodged answer

Make this sound like a real conversation, not an interrogation.
```

## Objection Handling Prompts

### Prompt 6: Objection Handling Playbook

```
Build an objection handling playbook for [product/service].

List the 10 most common objections and for each provide:
1. Acknowledge (validate their concern without agreeing it's a dealbreaker)
2. Clarify (one question to understand the real objection underneath)
3. Respond (your counter — use Feel/Felt/Found if appropriate)
4. Confirm (close the loop — "Does that address your concern?")

Objections to cover:
- "It's too expensive"
- "We're happy with our current solution"
- "We don't have budget right now"
- "I need to think about it"
- "Send me some information"
- [add your specific objections]
```

---

### Prompt 7: Price Objection Handler

```
My prospect just said: "Your price is too high. [Competitor] does the same thing for less."

Help me respond in a way that:
1. Doesn't panic or immediately discount
2. Reframes value vs. price
3. Surfaces what "same thing" actually means to them
4. Positions our differentiator without attacking the competitor
5. Advances the deal rather than defending

Give me 3 different response approaches for different prospect types.
```

## Proposal and Closing Prompts

### Prompt 8: Executive Proposal

```
Write an executive summary proposal for:

Prospect company: [company name and what they do]
My company: [your company]
Their problem: [specific problem you discussed]
My solution: [how you solve it]
Expected outcomes: [measurable results they get]
Investment: [$X]
Timeline: [implementation timeline]

Write a 1-page executive summary that:
- Opens with their problem in their own words
- Shows we understand their situation
- Presents the solution concisely
- Quantifies expected ROI or outcome
- Closes with a clear next step
```

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will prospects know my emails were written by AI?

If you use them as templates without personalizing, possibly. Always customize the [PERSONALIZATION] sections with real, specific details about the prospect. A well-personalized AI-assisted email is indistinguishable from a hand-written one — and far better than most generic outreach.

### Which prompt in this list gives the fastest ROI?

The objection handling playbook (Prompt 6). Building it once takes 20 minutes, but every rep on your team can use it in every call from day one. It's the highest leverage sales asset most teams don't have.

### Can I use ChatGPT to prep for specific calls?

Yes. Use Prompt 2 (Lead Research Brief) before every important call. It gives you hypotheses to test, not facts to present — which is exactly the right mental model for discovery.

### How do I personalize AI-written outreach at scale?

Use ChatGPT to write 10 variations of your core template across different industries or pain points. Then pull the right template for each prospect and add 1–2 genuinely personalized sentences at the top. This is "personalization at scale" that actually works.

### Does AI replace sales reps?

No — it removes the time-consuming parts so reps can spend more time on relationships and strategy. Prospecting research, email drafting, proposal writing, call prep, CRM notes — these take 40–60% of a rep's time. AI cuts that dramatically, freeing time for actual selling.
