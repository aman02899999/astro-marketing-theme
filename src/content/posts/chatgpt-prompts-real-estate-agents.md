Real estate agents who use AI write better listings faster, follow up more consistently, and spend more time with clients. These prompts cover every written communication task in the real estate business.

## Property Listing Prompts

### Prompt 1: Compelling Property Description

```
Write a compelling property listing description for:

Property type: [single-family / condo / townhouse / commercial / land]
Address area: [neighborhood or area — no specific address]
Bedrooms/bathrooms: [X bed / X bath]
Square footage: [X sqft]
Lot size: [X sqft or acres]
Key features: [list 8–12 standout features]
Renovations or updates: [list with approximate year]
View or outdoor features: [yard / view / patio / pool]
Location highlights: [proximity to schools / transit / amenities]
Target buyer: [who will love this home]
Price: [$X — optional]

Description requirements:
- Open with the home's most compelling feature (not "Welcome to...")
- Paint a lifestyle picture, not just a spec list
- Use sensory language where appropriate
- Highlight the neighborhood and community
- 150–200 words for MLS, 300 words for website/marketing
- CTA at the end: schedule a showing
```

### Prompt 2: Listing Marketing Copy Suite

```
Create complete marketing copy for this listing:

Property: [brief description]
Asking price: [$X]
Target buyer: [first-time buyer / move-up / investor / luxury / etc.]

Generate:
1. MLS description (150 words, detail-focused)
2. Social media caption (Instagram — lifestyle-focused, 100 words)
3. Email subject line (5 options — for "new listing" email)
4. Email body (new listing announcement, 150 words)
5. Open house invitation (social or email, 75 words)
6. SMS to buyer leads (60 characters — invite to showing)
7. Headline for print flyer (under 10 words)
8. 3 feature callout boxes (headline + 1 sentence each)
```

## Client Communication Prompts

### Prompt 3: Buyer Consultation Script

```
Create a buyer consultation script for:

Buyer profile: [first-time buyer / experienced / investor / relocating]
Budget range: [$X–$Y]
Timeline: [X months]
Must-haves they've shared: [list]
My market: [city or region]

Script sections:
1. Rapport and needs discovery (questions to ask — not a monologue)
2. Setting realistic expectations (market conditions, competition, process)
3. My value proposition (why work with me vs. a discount agent)
4. The process explained (offer to close in plain language)
5. The buyer agency agreement conversation
6. Next steps and commitments from both sides

Include: the 5 questions every buyer should answer before seeing a single home.
```

---

### Prompt 4: Market Report Writer

```
Write a monthly market report for:

Market: [city or neighborhood]
Time period: [month/year]
My audience: [past clients / social followers / farming list]

Data I have (paste your MLS stats):
- Median sale price: [$X] vs. last month [$Y] and last year [$Z]
- Days on market: [X] average
- List-to-sale ratio: [X%]
- Active listings: [X]
- New listings: [X]
- Sold: [X]
- Absorption rate: [X months]

Write a 300-word market commentary that:
- Leads with the most interesting trend
- Explains what the numbers mean for buyers and sellers
- Gives my expert interpretation (not just the stats)
- Includes a call to action (thinking of selling? / looking to buy?)
- Is interesting to read, not just informative
```

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What listing description mistakes do most agents make?

Starting with "Welcome to" (generic), listing features without benefits ("3 bedrooms" vs. "three spacious bedrooms"), using overused words ("stunning," "gorgeous," "rare find"), and forgetting to describe the lifestyle and neighborhood. Prompt 1 fixes all four by starting with the most compelling feature and painting a picture of living there.

### How do I generate leads with ChatGPT content?

Create genuinely useful local content: market reports (Prompt 4), neighborhood guides, first-time buyer education. Share consistently on social and email. ChatGPT reduces the time to produce this content from 2 hours to 20 minutes — making consistency achievable. Lead generation from content is a 6–18 month play; start now.

### Can I use ChatGPT listings if I haven't toured the property?

Only with information from the property profile and features provided by the seller. Never fabricate details. ChatGPT works with what you give it — your job is to provide accurate property information; ChatGPT's job is to make it compelling. Always verify descriptions with the seller before publishing.

### How do I make my real estate social media stand out?

Local specificity. Generic real estate content ("5 tips for first-time buyers") is everywhere. Content about your specific market — "What $600K buys you in [neighborhood] right now" with real examples — gets engagement from people who live there or are considering it. Use Prompt 2's social caption formula and add a local hook specific to the property or area.

### What's the best use of ChatGPT for a busy real estate agent?

Email follow-up sequences. Most agents lose deals not from bad service but from inconsistent follow-up. ChatGPT can draft personalized follow-up emails in 30 seconds — for buyer inquiries, post-showing check-ins, and past client touches — that would otherwise get skipped in a busy week.
