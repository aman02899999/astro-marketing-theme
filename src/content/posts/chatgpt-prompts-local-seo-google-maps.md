Local SEO is one of the highest-ROI investments a local business can make. When someone searches "plumber near me" or "best dentist in [city]," being in the top 3 Google Maps results can mean the difference between a full calendar and an empty one.

These prompts help you dominate local search — from Google Business Profile optimization to local landing pages and review generation.

## Google Business Profile Prompts

### Prompt 1: GBP Business Description

```
Write an optimized Google Business Profile description for:

Business name: [name]
Business type: [category]
Location: [city, state]
Services offered: [list main services]
Years in business: [X years]
Unique differentiators: [what makes you different]
Target keywords: [local keywords you want to rank for]

Requirements:
- 750 characters maximum
- Include primary keyword naturally in first sentence
- Include city name at least twice
- Highlight top 3 benefits for local customers
- End with a soft CTA
- Do NOT use "we" as the first word
```

---

### Prompt 2: GBP Post Calendar

```
Create a 4-week Google Business Profile post calendar for:

Business: [type of business]
Location: [city]
Goals: [drive calls / drive foot traffic / promote offer]

Week 1: What's New post (service highlight or news)
Week 2: Offer post (special promotion)
Week 3: Event post (if applicable) or Update post
Week 4: Product highlight or team spotlight

For each post:
- Post type
- 150-word copy (keyword-rich)
- CTA button type and text
- Image description / suggestion
```

## Local Landing Page Prompts

### Prompt 3: City-Specific Landing Page

```
Write a city-specific service landing page for:

Business: [type]
Service: [specific service]
City: [city, state]
Target keyword: "[service] in [city]" or "[service] [city]"
Competitors to differentiate from: [1–2 local competitors or "not specified"]
USP: [why choose you]

Structure:
1. H1 with city + service keyword
2. Intro paragraph (100 words, local focus)
3. Service description section
4. Why choose us (local differentiators)
5. Local social proof (template for inserting reviews)
6. Service area coverage (nearby towns/neighborhoods)
7. FAQ (5 local-intent questions)
8. CTA section with phone number and address prominence

Include LocalBusiness schema markup at the end in JSON-LD.
```

---

### Prompt 4: Multi-Location Page Strategy

```
I have a [type of business] with [X] locations across [region/state].

Help me create a multi-location SEO strategy:
Locations: [list city names]

For each location, define:
1. Target keyword cluster (primary + 5 secondary)
2. Unique content angle (what makes that location page different)
3. Local landmarks/neighborhoods to mention for relevance
4. Local schema markup fields to populate

Also provide:
- URL structure recommendation
- Internal linking strategy between locations
- How to avoid duplicate content across similar pages
```

## Review Generation Prompts

### Prompt 5: Review Request Templates

```
Write review request templates for a [type of business] to send after service completion.

Create 4 versions:
1. SMS (under 160 characters)
2. Email (under 100 words)
3. In-person verbal script (what to say at checkout/end of appointment)
4. Follow-up email if no review after 5 days

Each version should:
- Feel genuine, not automated
- Make leaving a review feel easy (not pressured)
- Include a direct link placeholder [REVIEW LINK]
- Mention the platform (Google) specifically
```

---

### Prompt 6: Review Response Templates

```
Write review response templates for a [type of business] covering:

Positive review (5 stars): 3 variations
Positive review mentioning staff by name: 1 variation
Neutral review (3 stars, no specific complaint): 2 variations
Negative review (legitimate complaint): 2 variations
Negative review (unfair or mistaken identity): 1 variation

Requirements for all responses:
- Thank by name if possible
- Mention the business name (for SEO)
- Address specific points mentioned
- Keep under 150 words
- Include a subtle CTA for negative reviews (offline resolution)
```

## Local Citation and Link Building Prompts

Build a local citation strategy for:

Business: [name, type, location]
Current citations I know about: [list or "none"]

Provide:
1. Top 20 citation directories for [business type] in [country/region]

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does local SEO take to show results?

Google Business Profile optimizations can show results in 2–4 weeks. Landing page and citation building typically takes 3–6 months to meaningfully impact rankings. Review accumulation is continuous — start immediately.

### Does having more reviews always help rankings?

Yes, both quantity and quality matter. Google uses review signals as a local ranking factor. Aim for consistent acquisition over time rather than a sudden burst. A steady stream of recent reviews outperforms 50 old ones with nothing new.

### What's the single most important local SEO action?

Completing and optimizing your Google Business Profile. It's free, directly controlled by you, and has the highest impact on local map pack rankings. Prompt 1 and 2 address this directly.

### How do I outrank a competitor with more reviews?

Proximity, relevance, and prominence are the three Google local ranking factors. Reviews are just one component of prominence. If a competitor has more reviews, focus on: being geographically closer to searchers (proximity), having more relevant category and service targeting (relevance), and building more citations and local links (prominence).

### Should every local business have city-specific landing pages?

Yes, if you serve multiple cities or neighborhoods. One well-optimized page per city you actively serve. Don't create pages for cities you don't serve just to capture traffic — Google detects thin, irrelevant local pages and ignores them.
