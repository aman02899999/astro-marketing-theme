YouTube Shorts is one of the fastest-growing content surfaces online — and the algorithm aggressively promotes Shorts to help creators reach new audiences. These prompts help you produce Shorts consistently and strategically.

## Shorts Script Prompts

### Prompt 1: Shorts Hook and Script

```
Write a YouTube Shorts script:

Topic: "[topic]"
Niche: [your channel niche]
Target viewer: [who you're making this for]
Key insight or fact: [the one thing you want them to know]
Call to action: [subscribe / watch the full video / comment]

Shorts formula (under 60 seconds = 150 words spoken):

HOOK (0–3 seconds): [Start mid-action or mid-statement — NO intro, NO "hey guys"]
BODY (3–45 seconds): [deliver the value fast — one idea, specific examples]
RETENTION HOOK (around 30 sec): ["And the craziest part is..." / "But here's what most people miss:"]
CTA (last 5 seconds): [one action only]

Include:
- [ON SCREEN TEXT] for key points
- [VISUAL CUT] suggestions every 3–5 seconds
- Pacing notes (short sentences = faster delivery)
```

### Prompt 2: Shorts Series Planner

```
Plan a YouTube Shorts series for my channel about: [niche]

Channel goal: [grow subscribers / reach new audience / drive long video views]
Series concept: [e.g. "Daily coding tips", "60-second history facts", "Quick business lessons"]
Posting frequency: [X Shorts per week]
Series length: [X episodes]

For each episode provide:
- Episode number and title
- Core hook (first 3 seconds)
- One-sentence summary of content
- CTA direction (subscribe / watch full video on [topic])
- Hook to next episode (how to tease the series)

Series architecture:
- Episode 1: high-value "best" episode (puts best foot forward for algorithm)
- Episodes 2–X: build on established theme
- Occasional: "watch the full video" bridge episode

Also: how to cross-promote this Shorts series in my long-form videos.
```

```
Convert this long video content into YouTube Shorts:

Long video topic: "[title]"
Key moments from the video:
[Paste timestamps and descriptions of your best moments, OR summarize key points]
Video URL/description: [for context]

Identify and script 5 Shorts from this content:

For each Short:
1. Title suggestion (for Shorts title)
2. The specific moment or insight to feature
3. Script (under 60 seconds, optimized for Shorts format)
4. Hook line (first 3 seconds — must work without context from the long video)
5. CTA: "Watch the full video: [topic]" — drives traffic to long video

Rule: each Short must be completely understandable without watching the parent video.
```

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do YouTube Shorts help or hurt long-form video performance?

Properly executed Shorts help long-form by: (1) reaching new audiences who then subscribe, (2) specifically driving traffic to related long-form videos via CTAs, (3) improving overall channel velocity (more uploads = more algorithm signals). Shorts that attract an audience expecting only short content can dilute watch time metrics. The key is attracting the right subscribers with your Shorts.

### What Shorts content goes viral most consistently?

Content with immediate utility ("do this" tips), surprising or counterintuitive facts, identity-affirming content ("every [type] will understand this"), and pattern interrupts (starts in an unexpected place). The first 3 seconds are the only thing that matters — if viewers swipe, nothing else counts.

### How long should a YouTube Short actually be?

The algorithm doesn't favor longer or shorter — it favors completion rate. A 30-second Short with 90% completion beats a 59-second Short with 40% completion. Script for the minimum time needed to deliver the value. Most topics can be covered in 20–45 seconds; don't pad to hit a length.

### Should I use Shorts to grow a new channel?

Yes — especially in 2025 when YouTube is heavily promoting Shorts to compete with TikTok. A new channel can reach thousands of viewers in the first week through Shorts that would take months via long-form. Use Shorts to build an audience, then convert them to long-form viewers with strategic CTAs.

### How many Shorts should I post per week for growth?

Three to five per week for rapid growth. The Shorts algorithm responds to frequency — consistent daily posting during a 30-day sprint can dramatically accelerate channel velocity. After establishing momentum, 3 per week sustains growth. Batch-produce a week's Shorts in a single session to make this sustainable.
