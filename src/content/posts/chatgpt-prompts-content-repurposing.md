Creating original content from scratch every day is unsustainable. The most efficient content marketers create once and distribute everywhere — one core piece of content becomes 10, 15, or 20 pieces across multiple platforms. These prompts automate the repurposing engine.

## Core Repurposing Prompts

### Prompt 1: The Full Repurpose Stack

```
Repurpose this piece of content into every format:

ORIGINAL CONTENT:
[paste your blog post, podcast transcript, or video script]

Source type: [blog post / podcast / video / webinar / research report]
Brand: [description]
Audience: [who consumes your content]
Brand voice: [3 adjectives]

Repurpose into ALL of the following:

SOCIAL MEDIA:
1. LinkedIn post (hook + 5 insight bullets + question)
2. Twitter/X thread (8 tweets, first is the hook)
3. Instagram carousel (8 slides — headline + 25 words per slide)
4. Facebook post (conversational, 150 words)
5. 3 standalone quote graphics (one punchy sentence each)

EMAIL:
6. Newsletter section (200 words — for an existing newsletter)
7. Subject line variations (5 options for an email about this topic)

SHORT VIDEO:
8. TikTok/Reels hook script (first 5 seconds spoken + on-screen text)
9. YouTube Shorts script (45–60 seconds)

LONG-FORM:
10. Email sequence (3-email drip on this topic)

SEO:
11. FAQ section (5 questions from this content, 50 words each)
12. Meta description for the original piece (155 characters)
```

### Prompt 2: Podcast to Everything

```
I have this podcast episode transcript. Repurpose it:

TRANSCRIPT:
[paste transcript — or summarize the episode]

Episode: [title]
Guest (if applicable): [name and expertise]
Key insights: [list 3–5 if you want to guide it]

Produce:
1. Episode show notes (400 words — for the podcast website)
2. Audiogram script (best 60-second quote from the episode for social)
3. LinkedIn post from the guest's perspective (if applicable)
4. Twitter thread: "X insights from my conversation with [guest]"
5. Blog post outline (turn the interview into a searchable article)
6. Email to send to subscribers (announce + key takeaway + link)
7. YouTube chapter titles (if posting the video)
8. Pull quote graphics (3 quotes, formatted for Instagram)
```

### Prompt 3: Video to Written Content

```
Turn this YouTube video [or webinar / recorded presentation] into written content:

VIDEO CONTENT SUMMARY OR TRANSCRIPT:
[paste transcript or describe the video]

Video title: "[title]"
Duration: [X minutes]
Main topic: [topic]

Produce:
1. Long-form blog post (1,200–1,500 words) — SEO-optimized version of the video
2. Summary article (400 words) — for people who won't watch the full video
3. Key takeaways listicle (top 7 insights, bullet format)
4. Email newsletter (3 paragraphs + link to video)
5. Social caption for sharing the video link (LinkedIn + Twitter versions)
6. Slide deck outline (if you want to present this content)
7. Downloadable PDF guide outline (turn video into a lead magnet)
```

## Scaling Prompts

### Prompt 4: Content Multiplication System

```
Design a content multiplication system for:

My primary content format: [the format you create most naturally — long blog / podcast / video]
Publishing frequency: [X per month]
Platforms I want to be on: [list]
Team size: [just me / small team]
Time available for distribution: [X hours per week]

Create:
1. A "content tree" showing how one primary piece branches into secondary pieces
2. Which secondary formats to prioritize (highest ROI for my audience + platforms)
3. A production checklist for each primary piece (what to repurpose every time)
4. Templates to speed up each repurpose (what's the same every time)
5. Batching strategy (repurpose a week's secondary content from one primary session)
6. Time estimate per format (realistic, not aspirational)
```

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Doesn't repurposed content feel repetitive to followers who see everything?

Most followers see less than 10% of what you post. Different platforms also have different audiences. The 0.5% who follow you everywhere and notice the repurposing are far outnumbered by the 99.5% who only see one platform. Consistent core messages across platforms actually builds recognition and trust.

### Which formats should I prioritize when repurposing?

Start with the two highest-ROI formats for your audience: typically email (most direct access to your audience) and your strongest social platform. Build the habit of always producing those two, then add others as bandwidth allows. Prompt 4 helps you design this system based on your specific constraints.

### Does repurposed content rank on Google?

The blog post version of a podcast or video (Prompt 2 and 3) can rank well if it's genuinely comprehensive and optimized. Don't just transcribe — restructure, add headings, add FAQ sections, and optimize for the target keyword. A well-adapted blog post based on strong original content often ranks better than content written purely for SEO.

### How do I repurpose old content that's still relevant?

Identify your evergreen content — pieces that are as useful today as when published. Update the statistics and examples, refresh the intro to remove dated references, and run the updated piece through Prompt 1. A well-performing 2-year-old post refreshed and repurposed often drives more traffic than new content from scratch.

### What's the single highest-ROI repurposing action?

Turning your top-performing blog posts into email sequences (Prompt 1, item 10). Your best organic content is your best-proven content — the topics people search for and engage with most. Turning those into nurture sequences means every new email subscriber gets your best thinking, not just your newest.
