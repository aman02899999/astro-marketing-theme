Podcasting rewards consistency and content quality above all else. These prompts remove the bottlenecks — episode outlines, interview prep, show notes, and titles — so you can focus on the conversations and stories that make great audio.

## Episode Planning Prompts

### Prompt 1: Solo Episode Outline

```
Create an outline for a solo podcast episode:

Show: [podcast name and topic]
Episode topic: [specific subject]
Target listener: [who listens]
Episode goal: [what you want them to think, feel, or do after listening]
Estimated length: [X minutes]
My unique angle: [your specific perspective or experience]

Outline structure:
1. HOOK (first 60 seconds): the most compelling way to open — not "welcome back to the show"
2. EPISODE PREVIEW: tease the 3 main points
3. SECTION 1 [title]: main point + evidence + story or example
4. SECTION 2 [title]: main point + evidence + story or example
5. SECTION 3 [title]: main point + evidence + story or example
6. ACTIONABLE TAKEAWAY: the one thing they should do after listening
7. CLOSE: recap + next episode tease + CTA (subscribe / review / share)

Timestamps: [estimated minutes for each section]
```

### Prompt 2: Guest Interview Question Guide

```
Prepare interview questions for:

Guest: [name and expertise]
Their background: [what they've done / what they're known for]
My show: [topic and audience]
Interview goal: [what my listeners should get from this episode]
Angle: [what aspect of this person's story I want to explore]

Question categories:

OPENING (establish context):
- 2 questions to introduce who they are in their own words

THEIR JOURNEY (the narrative):
- 3 questions about how they got here (look for the turning points, failures, decisions)

CORE EXPERTISE (the value):
- 5 questions on the specific topic my audience needs
- For each: include a follow-up probe

CONTRARIAN / HARD QUESTION:
- 2 questions that challenge conventional wisdom or their past statements

CLOSE:
- What's one thing people get wrong about [topic]?
- What would you tell your 25-year-old self?
- Where can listeners find you?

Also: the question NOT to ask (too common / too generic / every podcaster asks it).
```

## Show Notes Prompts

### Prompt 3: Show Notes Writer

```
Write show notes for this podcast episode:

Episode title: "[title]"
Guest (if applicable): [name and bio]
Episode summary: [paste your rough notes or transcript highlights]
Key topics covered: [list main subjects discussed]
Resources mentioned: [any books, tools, websites mentioned]
Episode length: [X minutes]
Platform links: [where to listen — leave as placeholders]

Show notes structure:
1. Episode summary (2 paragraphs — compelling, not just descriptive)
2. What you'll learn in this episode (3–5 bullets)
3. Guest bio (if applicable — 3 sentences, third-person)
4. Episode highlights with timestamps
5. Resources mentioned (formatted list with links)
6. Key quotes from the episode (3 pull quotes)
7. Connect with guest/host (social links placeholder)
8. Subscribe and review CTA

SEO: include the episode's primary keyword 2–3 times naturally.
Length: 400–600 words.
```

### Prompt 4: Episode Title Generator

```
Write episode title options for:

Episode topic: [what the episode is about]
Guest (if applicable): [name]
Key insight or story from the episode: [the most compelling thing discussed]
Show tone: [educational / storytelling / interview / news / conversational]
Audience: [who listens]

Generate 15 title options in these styles:
1. Guest name + insight (5 titles — for interview episodes)
2. Topic-first (5 titles — good for SEO, searchable)
3. Story/intrigue (5 titles — curiosity-driven)

For each: rate it on [searchability 1–10] and [clickability 1–10].
Flag top 3 to A/B test.

Title rules:
- Under 60 characters for full display in podcast apps
- No "EP 47:" at the start (leads waste)
- No "with [Guest Name]" — integrate naturally
```

## Growth and Monetization Prompts

### Prompt 5: Podcast Launch Plan

```
Create a podcast launch plan for:

Podcast: [name and concept]
Target audience: [who you're making this for]
Launch date: [target]
My platform: [Spotify / Apple / all platforms via Anchor / Buzzsprout / etc.]
Episodes ready at launch: [X episodes — best practice is 3–5]
My existing audience: [email list / social following / community — or "starting from zero"]

90-day launch plan:
Pre-launch (4 weeks out):
- Content preparation milestones
- Platform setup checklist
- Audience building teaser strategy

Launch week:
- Day-by-day launch activities
- Launch email / social posts
- Ask for early reviews strategy (critical for Apple Podcasts ranking)

Post-launch (weeks 2–12):
- Content cadence
- Guest outreach strategy
- Community building
- First monetization steps
```

### Prompt 6: Podcast Sponsorship Pitch

```
Write a sponsorship pitch for my podcast:

Podcast: [name]
Topic: [niche]
Episodes published: [X]
Average downloads per episode: [X]
Listener demographics: [age, profession, interests — if known]
Sponsor category I'm targeting: [type of brand that fits my audience]

Media kit sections:
1. Show overview (3 sentences — what it's about and who listens)
2. Audience profile (demographics + why they're valuable to this sponsor)
3. Download metrics (weekly/monthly downloads, growth rate)
4. Sponsorship packages:
   - Pre-roll (30 seconds): [$X]
   - Mid-roll (60 seconds): [$X]
   - Exclusive sponsor: [$X]
5. What past sponsors/testimonials say (or omit if none)
6. Contact and booking

Pricing formula: [Your average downloads × $25 CPM = suggested rate]
```

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many episodes should I have ready before launching?

Three to five minimum. This gives listeners something to binge-listen, demonstrates you're committed (not a one-episode experiment), and gives the Apple Podcasts algorithm more content to evaluate. Launching with one episode is the most common podcasting mistake — listeners who find your show and enjoy it want more immediately.

### What microphone should a beginner podcaster use?

The Audio-Technica ATR2100x-USB or Samson Q2U (both USB and XLR) are the standard beginner recommendations at $80–$100. They eliminate 90% of room noise issues, sound professional out of the box, and work with any computer. More expensive equipment rarely improves a mediocre recording environment — treat your room first (carpet, curtains, bookshelves) before upgrading gear.

### How long should podcast episodes be?

As long as the content deserves — not a minute longer. Most successful podcasts run 25–45 minutes for interview formats and 15–25 minutes for solo educational shows. Listener analytics consistently show drop-off increases significantly past 45 minutes for most genres. Let content length drive episode length, then edit ruthlessly.

### How do I get my podcast on Spotify and Apple Podcasts?

Use a podcast hosting platform (Buzzsprout, Anchor/Spotify for Podcasters, Transistor, Podbean) that automatically distributes to all major platforms. You don't submit directly to Apple or Spotify — your host does it for you. Most hosts have a free tier. Prompt 5's launch plan includes the platform setup checklist.

### When can I realistically expect to monetize a podcast?

Most podcasters see first sponsorship revenue between 500–1,000 downloads per episode (achievable in 3–12 months with consistent effort). Affiliate revenue can start at any download count if your audience is highly targeted. Memberships (Patreon, Supercast) work at any size if your community values exclusivity. Don't wait for a large audience to think about monetization structure — design it in from day one.
